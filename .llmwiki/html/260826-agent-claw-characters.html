What actually changed, not just recolored: no square body, no antenna, no visor-strip. The claws are real two-finger pincers built from paths (not a stroked line over a blob, which read as a scratch mark in the first attempt), the body is a soft dome instead of a rounded square, and each prop is held up in the claw rather than pinned to a flat panel on the chest. Built and checked one character at a time against a plain background before assembling this page, specifically because the last two rounds looked fine in my head and wrong on screen.
