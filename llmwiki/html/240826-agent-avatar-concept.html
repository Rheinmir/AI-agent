Why this shape

Two claw tips instead of antennae — a quiet nod to the "Clawatar" naming rabbit hole that started this. A visor with one teal dot reads as "paying attention" without the generic two-round-eyes-and-eyelashes cute-bot look. The cursor mouth ties it to a coding agent specifically, not a generic AI blob.

Palette

Amber body, ink outline, one teal accent — deliberately not the usual purple-blue AI gradient.
